# Future Features (Beyond Phase 1 MVP)

This document outlines features that are officially out of scope for the first release, but the system architecture is designed to accommodate them when the platform matures.

## Phase 2: Engagement & Trust

* **Rating & Reviews System:** Let customers leave a star rating and a review on a workshop's profile after physical work is completed.
* **Favorites (Wishlist):** Allow returning customers (this would require creating Customer Accounts) to save designs they like in a "Favorites" list.
* **Advanced Statistics:** Show owners their Dashboard how many views their phone number received and how many people viewed each post.

## Phase 3: Operations & Internal Tools

* **Internal Messaging:** Build a Real-Time chat system where customers can talk to the blacksmith directly on the platform instead of immediately jumping to WhatsApp.
* **Cost/Material Calculator:** A specialized tool inside the dashboard where the blacksmith inputs dimensions of a door and current steel prices, and it outputs an estimated cost of materials vs profit.
* **Project Status Tracker:** Allow the owner to update the status of a project for a specific customer (e.g. "Cutting Iron", "Painting", "Ready for Installation").

## Phase 4: E-Commerce & Advanced Tech

* **Online Payments:** For fixed-price items like metal decor or furniture, allow checkout through the platform with a payment gateway (Stripe, local gateways).
* **AI Image Search:** Allowing a customer to upload a picture of a door they saw in the street, and using AI to present visually similar works available on the platform.
* **Mobile Applications:** Building native or cross-platform (Flutter) apps utilizing the REST API.
