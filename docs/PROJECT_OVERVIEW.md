# Project Overview

## Project Idea

The project is a **Marketplace platform specialized in blacksmithery and ironworks**.
The platform acts as a bridge connecting two main categories:

1. **Blacksmith Workshop Owners:** To showcase their works and models, and reach a larger number of customers.
2. **Customers & Seekers:** To browse different models of blacksmithing works instead of random searching, compare designs, and then contact the specialized workshops.

## Primary Goal of the Platform (Phase 1 MVP)

Showcase, discover, and connect.
The platform is not considered an E-commerce store in its first phase (no online payment or shopping cart). Rather, it is an **advanced Portfolio system for blacksmith workshops** that makes it easy for the customer to find what they are looking for.

## Target Audience

- **Workshop Owners & Freelancers:** Who do not have their own website and want a ready-to-use platform to display their work professionally.
- **Contractors & Homeowners (Customers):** Who are looking for doors, windows, awnings, handrails, or any iron furniture, and want to see previous pictures and details of models before making a deal.

## Core Components for the First Version

1. **Public Portal:** For browsing works and models by category.
2. **Workshop Profile:** A public page for each business owner containing contact information and all their works.
3. **Owner Dashboard:** To manage posts, images, and workshop details.
4. **Admin Dashboard:** To monitor content, manage users, and manage categories.
