# User Roles

The system in its first version contains three main categories of users:

## 1. Customer / Guest

The primary user of the public interface of the platform, who is looking for designs and models or looking for a workshop for execution.

* **Access:** Public interface of the website (Frontend).
* **Permissions:**
  * Browse the homepage.
  * Browse work categories (Doors, Awnings..).
  * Search and filter.
  * View work details and model pictures.
  * View workshop and owner data.
  * Click on contact buttons (WhatsApp, phone call).
  * Submit a report regarding abusive or inaccurate content.
* **Limitations:** The visitor does not need an account in the first phase, and has no permissions to edit or add anything.

## 2. Workshop Owner / Blacksmith

The platform partner who owns a workshop or provides blacksmithing services, and uses the platform as a gallery for their work.

* **Access:** Workshop Owner Dashboard + public interface.
* **Permissions:**
  * Register an account, confirm it, and manage login data.
  * Customize the workshop profile (Name, description, logo, location, contact numbers).
  * Create new projects/posts for executed works or models upon request.
  * Upload pictures of the works.
  * Freely edit their posts, prices, and details.
  * Delete posts.
  * See simple statistics (in the future) such as the number of visits to their page.
* **Limitations:**
  * Only has the right to dispose of data and posts belonging to their workshop.
  * Does not have the right to add main sections to the platform (Categories).

## 3. Administrator

The manager responsible for the entire platform, controlling all general settings and oversight.

* **Access:** Admin Dashboard.
* **Permissions:**
  * Add, edit, and delete basic work categories.
  * Monitor all posts added to the platform, with the ability to delete or hide any post that violates the terms.
  * Manage users (workshops) and suspend or activate accounts.
  * View reports received from customers and take action.
  * View comprehensive platform statistics (number of workshops, number of posts, etc.).
* **Limitations:**
  * It is preferred to have an Activity Log that records sensitive admin actions.
